AI Alt Text Generator

The Product

ImageAltAI is an AI Image SEO Tool built keeping SEO pros and beginners both in mind; who don’t want 42 clicks to write one alt tag.

It creates SEO-friendly Alt text, Captions, and File names for images. You can upload one image (like most alternative Image Alt Tools let). Or, up to 15 in bulk (only a couple tools let do). We go a step further and let just paste a live URL - fetch its images - and suggest image metadata, unlike any other (as of Dec '25). We call it Page Scan feature.

Perfect for blogs, SaaS, eCommerce, agencies, and anyone who wants search engines, answer engines, and LLMs to understand their images. Plus, in line with W3C (and plausibly your local government's) Web Accessibility Initiative.

And... it’s Free! No signup. No daily limits.

Reason to Build

Why?

Because most images online are basically whispering “figure me out” to Google and ChatGPT. Manual metadata writing is the kind of task only free-loading SEO interns enjoy.

We saw creators, marketers, and businesses skip alt text not because they don’t care — but because the process is unnecessarily painful.

So we built a tool that does the heavy lifting in seconds, stays accurate, and accessible even if you’ve never heard of Neil Patel.

Methodology to Build

How?

Our AI analyzes each image, understands what’s happening in it, and generates metadata based on real-world SEO best practices.

The backend uses OpenAI’s latest models, prompt-engineered for higher accuracy, keyword sensitivity, and context awareness — whether it’s a single hero image or a full gallery.

The result? Consistent, clean, SEO-ready metadata with a UI simple enough for beginners and fast enough for power users.
