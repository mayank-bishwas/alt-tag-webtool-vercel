    FAQs on Bulk SEO Optimization of Images

    What is SEO?

    Search engine optimization is the process of improving the quality and quantity of website traffic to a website or a web page from search engines. SEO targets unpaid search traffic rather than direct traffic, referral traffic, social media traffic, or paid traffic.

    How does alt text help SEO?

    Alt text helps search engines understand image content. It improves accessibility, relevance, and can boost image search rankings.

    How do I name image files for SEO?

    Use descriptive, keyword-rich, lowercase file names separated by hyphens. Example: “golden-retriever-dog-running.jpg”.
