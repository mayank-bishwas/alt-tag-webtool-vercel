    It’s the process of optimizing multiple images at once through alt text, captions, file names, and contextual metadata so search engines (and LLMs/Answer Engines) can better understand them. Bulk SEO also helps keep your entire gallery or media set consistent and accessible as per W3C (and often your local government’s) web accessibility guidelines.
