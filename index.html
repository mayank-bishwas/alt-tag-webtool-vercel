It’s the practice of helping search engines (and also LLMs and Answer Engines) understand your images through alt text, captions, file names, and context. Further, image SEO best practices help make your website more user-friendly in accordance with the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) (and possibly your local government's) guidelines of web accessibility.
